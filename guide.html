01 · Global Settings & Businesses

Build the system masters first

These are the reference records used by Registration, Movement, Service, Documents, R2R and the rest of IMS.

What is Global Settings?

Global Settings contains the controlled values used repeatedly across IMS, such as warehouses, item types, units, categories, sizes, grades, PPF, connections, ranges, brands, models and weight units.

What are Businesses?

Businesses are the external companies IMS works with. A business may act as a Supplier, Client, Service Provider or another supported business role. Use one clean business record instead of entering company names differently in every transaction.

02 · Register / Import Items

Create the item before anything can happen to it

Registration gives each physical item its permanent identity and ownership information. Owned and R2R items follow different ownership rules.

Owned / New Registration

Use this for Wellora-owned physical items. Each physical serialized item should have its own Wellora SN. Excel import/export is the preferred bulk workflow.

R2R Registration

Use this for supplier-owned Rent-to-Rent items. Each physical item uses its R2R SN, supplier and supplier-related PO information. The item remains supplier-owned throughout its lifecycle.

Download / Prepare ExcelUse the IMS column structure. Do not rename headers.
→
One Physical SN Per RowQty should represent one permanent physical serialized item.
→
ImportIMS validates required fields, masters and serial numbers.
→
Resolve ErrorsFix duplicate SNs or unknown master values before continuing.
→
Confirm Item ExistsCheck the item in Stock Inventory after successful registration.
Duplicate protection: Wellora SN / R2R SN is the unique operational identity. Do not intentionally reuse an existing serial number.
R2R: Imported R2R items intended for a warehouse use the normal incoming process. They can be In Transit until the arrival step is completed; do not manually force them to Available.
03 · Movements

Record every physical location change

Movement is the normal module for supplier, warehouse and client transfers. If the item physically changes location, the movement should exist in IMS.

Receive from Supplier

Supplier → Warehouse. Used when an eligible item is received into a warehouse. Arrival normally makes the item Available.

Transfer Warehouse

Warehouse → Warehouse. Used for internal transfer between physical inventory locations.

Rental / Send to Client

Warehouse → Client. Used when stock is sent to a client. Client PO is the normal reference.

Return from Client

Client → Warehouse. Use this when equipment physically comes back from a client location.

Fast Pick or Browse

Use Wellora SN / R2R SN when known, or browse eligible items. IMS only presents items that meet the source/status rules for that movement.

Never skip arrival. Starting a movement and physically receiving the item are two different events. If it has arrived, complete the arrival step so Stock Inventory reflects reality.
04 · Service Cycle

Track inspection, maintenance and workshop activity

Service Cycle records work performed on an item while preserving its identity, location history and availability status.
Select Service TypeChoose the correct service activity.
→
Select SourceWarehouse, Client or Supplier as allowed.
→
Add Item(s)Fast Pick or Browse eligible stock.
→
Start ServiceIMS records the active service cycle.
→
Complete ServiceRecord Pass / Failed and return steps if required.

Pass

Use when the service requirement is completed satisfactorily. Follow any required return/arrival step for off-site work.

Failed

Use when the item should not return to normal available use. Failed items may remain Not Available until the next valid service or disposition action.

Rule: Do not create a second active service cycle for an item already under service. Finish or restart the existing service workflow correctly.
05 · Stock Inventory

Check the current truth of every item

Stock Inventory is where users confirm an item's current ownership, status and physical location after operations are completed.

What to check

Confirm Wellora SN / R2R SN, Description, ownership, quantity, Current Status, Current Location and other item specifications. Use inventory as the current operational view.

When to check it

After registration, arrival, service completion, reservation changes, incident recovery and disposition. If the displayed state is wrong, fix the operational workflow that caused it rather than manually inventing a new state.

Current state vs history: Stock Inventory tells you where the item is now. Movement, Service, Reservation, Incident, Disposition and operational logs explain how it got there.
06 · Documents

Connect operations to commercial references

Documents links operational activity to the client and supplier commercial references used by the business.

Client side

Use client commercial records to review client PO information and the items/activities connected to that reference.

Supplier side

Use supplier commercial records to review supplier PO information and the registered or operational items connected to that supplier reference.

Use consistent PO numbers. A mistyped PO may create a separate commercial reference instead of linking to the correct one.
Documents support operations. Movement and registration remain the authoritative physical stock actions.

Manage supplier-owned Rent-to-Rent stock

R2R keeps supplier ownership visible while the item is received, used, serviced and eventually returned to its owner.

08 · Reservation

Hold available warehouse stock for a planned client job

Reservation prevents selected Available items from being accidentally committed elsewhere before the planned client movement.
Select WarehouseChoose the stock location.
→

→
Add Available ItemsFast Pick or Browse Eligible Items.
→
ReserveItems become Reserved.
→
Use / ReleaseUse the reservation for delivery or release it when no longer needed.
Reserved is not delivered. Reservation only holds the stock. Use Movement when the item physically leaves the warehouse.
09 · Disposition

Record a permanent inventory exit

Disposition is for final outcomes such as sold, scrapped, written off, returned to supplier/owner or another approved permanent exit.

Before disposition

Confirm the item is physically in a valid state for disposal. It should not still be At Client, In Transit, Reserved or actively under Maintenance / Inspection.

Complete disposition

Find the exact item, select the correct final outcome, enter the relevant date/reference/counterparty and save the disposition.

Disposition is a terminal action. Do not use it as a shortcut for temporary unavailability, service failure, missing stock or a normal location transfer.
10 · Incident

Report Missing or Stolen items and record recovery

Incident protects the stock record when an item cannot be physically accounted for.

Report Incident

Find the exact item, choose the incident type, enter the event date/reference/remark and submit. IMS prevents a second active incident on the same item.

Recover Item

When the physical item is recovered, use the recovery action from the open incident. IMS restores the appropriate prior stock state where supported by the workflow.

Incident is not disposition. Missing or Stolen means the item may still be recovered. Use Disposition only when a separate approved permanent exit is required.
