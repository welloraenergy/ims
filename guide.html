01 · Getting Started

How to use IMS

IMS follows the physical item. Always select the correct source, destination and item before saving an operation.

1. Sign in

Use your authorised IMS account. Menus and actions depend on your assigned role.

2. Check item identity

Use Wellora SN / R2R SN whenever possible. Description search is also available.

3. Confirm operation

Review source, destination, quantity, reference and logistics information before submitting.

02 · Finding Items

Fast Pick or Browse

The same item-pick pattern is used across Movement, Service Cycle, Reservation, Disposition and Incident.

Fast Pick

If you know the Wellora SN / R2R SN, type it in the exact-value field and press Enter or Find. You may also search by exact Description.

Best for users who already know the serial number or are scanning items one by one.

Browse Eligible Items

After choosing the required source/location, IMS shows up to 20 eligible items per page. Use Previous / Next and select one or several items as allowed by the workflow.

IMS does not load hundreds of visible rows at once. The browser uses cursor pagination.
03 · Movement

Move items between locations

Use Movement whenever the physical item changes location between supplier, warehouse and client.
Select Movement TypeReceive, transfer, send to client or return from client.
→
Select SourceIMS checks which items are eligible at that source.
→
Select DestinationChoose the receiving warehouse or client.
→
Add ItemsFast Pick or Browse Eligible Items.
→
Start MovementItems become In Transit.
→
Mark ArrivedFinal status/location is written to inventory.

Receive from Supplier

Requires Wellora PO. Destination is warehouse. Arrival normally becomes Available.

Warehouse Transfer

Requires Internal Transfer No. Source and destination warehouses must be different.

Rental / Send to Client

Requires Client PO. May use an existing reservation. Arrival becomes At Client.

04 · Service Cycle

Inspection, maintenance and machine shop

A service cycle records work performed on items without losing their operational history.
Start ServiceSelect service type, source, provider and items.
→
Under ServiceItem status is controlled by the service workflow.
→
End ServiceRecord Pass or Failed.
→
Return if NeededOff-site items may require a return transit.
→
ArriveIMS restores the correct destination status.
Failed service items remain Not Available until the next valid action. Restart Failed Service keeps the item at its current service location.
05 · Reservation

Reserve warehouse stock for a client

Reservation locks selected Available stock so it cannot be used by another reservation or movement accidentally.
Select WarehouseOnly stock at this location is eligible.
→

→

→

→

07 · Incident

Missing, stolen and recovery

Use Incident when an item is missing or stolen. Recovery restores the previous inventory state where possible.

Report Incident

Find the exact item, choose Missing or Stolen, enter date/reference/remark and submit.

Recover Item

Use Recover Item from Open Incidents. IMS restores the previous stock balances and location recorded before the incident.

08 · Disposition

Permanent exit from active stock

Disposition is final. Use it only when the item is sold, scrapped, written off, returned to supplier or otherwise permanently exited.

Before disposition

The item must not still be At Client, In Transit, under Maintenance/Inspection or Reserved. Return or release it first.

Complete disposition

Find the item, select the outcome, enter date/reference/counterparty and save. IMS stores the terminal status and history.

09 · Documents

Client and supplier commercial references

Documents connects operational item history with commercial references such as client POs and supplier POs.

Client Documents

Review client PO information, linked items, quantities, periods and related commercial status.

Supplier Documents

Review supplier-side references associated with incoming stock and R2R ownership.

Linked Items

Documents are meant to show the items related to each commercial reference, individually or as a batch.

10 · Status Guide

Common inventory statuses

Status describes the current operational condition of stock. Location tells you where it physically belongs.
StatusMeaningTypical next action
AvailableReady for normal warehouse use.Movement, Service, Reservation or other valid operation.
At ClientPhysically with a client.Return from Client, Service where allowed, or continue rental.
ReservedWarehouse stock allocated to a reservation.Use the reservation in client delivery or cancel/release it.
In TransitMovement has started but arrival is not yet confirmed.Mark Arrived when the item reaches destination.
MaintenanceItem is under service or waiting for service return.End Service, Restart Failed Service or Return.
Not AvailableItem is present but not currently available for normal use.Resolve service/failure or other operational restriction.
Missing / StolenActive incident exists.Recover Item when found.
Disposed / ReturnedItem has permanently exited active stock.No normal operational movement.
Good Practice

Before you click Save

Identity: confirm the Wellora SN / R2R SN is the correct physical item.
Location: confirm the item is really at the selected source.
Reference: check PO, transfer, receiving, service or incident references carefully.
Arrival: do not leave completed physical movements stuck In Transit.
History: use IMS operations rather than manually changing inventory status whenever a workflow exists.
